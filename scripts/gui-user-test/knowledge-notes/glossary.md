# Team glossary

Short definitions of the words that come up in stand-up and in review.

- **Candidate** -- a build of the main branch that may become a release.
- **Captain** -- the teammate who promotes this week's candidate.
- **Soak** -- the day a candidate spends on staging before promotion.
- **Stand-up** -- the ten-minute daily sync at ten in the morning.
- **Buddy** -- the teammate paired with a newcomer for their first week.
- **Design note** -- a one-page description of a change, written before the code.
- **Incident** -- any user-visible outage, tracked until the write-up is published.
