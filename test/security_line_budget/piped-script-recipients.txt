A heredoc piped into a shell is read as its script whatever the shell's arguments hold:
`rm_floor._pipe_recipients` finds every command the pipe may feed (the next one read, and
the one starting first after it on its own line, which a quoted substitution in the
shell's words puts after its own commands) by bisecting sorted offsets, and
`_reads_pipe` says whether that command is a shell reading the pipe. No check is
relaxed.
