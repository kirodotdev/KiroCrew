`_plain_case_pattern_indices` in `argv_floor_ssh.py`: the ssh-to-self floor skips a
case arm's `)`-ending pattern word only when the whole case statement is plainly shaped
(`case WORD in`, arms of a pattern + simple words + `;;`, then a bare `esac`). Any other
shape reads every word as a possible verb, exactly as a line with no case, so the floor
is never looser than before for a glued `esac`, a redirect, an operator, a quoted `)`
filename or a mis-read `CASE` function. The helper, its reason comment and the one-line
docs update are the growth, plus four names the facade mirror forwards.
