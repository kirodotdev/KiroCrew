_fold_windows_alias folds a loopback admin share (\\localhost\C$ and its \\?\UNC\, \\.\UNC\ and \??\UNC\ forms, plus 127.x and ::1 hosts) to the local drive it opens, so every sensitive-path check that calls the fold sees the drive path. One compiled regex, _fold_loopback_admin_share (the prefix-only fold governance also applies to a path pattern) and _fold_windows_namespace (the fold without it, which governance matches a rule against too), so a fold only adds matches. The keystone folds each candidate form again, so a relative path anchored under a loopback-share base_dir or CWD meets the fence too.
